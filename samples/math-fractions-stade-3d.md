# Le Stade des Fractions — a MetaDAX sample

*An illustrative example made from a parent's request — nothing identifying. A way, never the way.*

**Guide:** a parent at home · **Learner:** un apprenant de 11 ans · **Age band:** 10–12 · **Subject:** Math · **Language:** French

## The package

A fractions course in French, in seven steps, set inside a single 3D stadium built with three.js. The learner arrives with no prior fractions and leaves having covered: what a fraction is (the pitch cut into equal parts), comparing fractions (two runners on the 400 m track), adding and subtracting (laps repainted with new equal parts), a fraction of a number (balls shared into hoops), finding the whole from a fraction — and a ten-penalty final to close. Done with a parent at home, in one sitting or across a few.

Play it here: <https://daxfoundation.org/metadax/samples/stade-des-fractions/>

## Each step

Every step has four parts: *Regarde* (a step-by-step 3D walkthrough), *Pourquoi ?* (the reason), *À toi !* (five questions on the stadium, two tries, then the answer and why), and *Guide* (what to say, what to ask, what to watch for, an off-screen activity, the answer key). A *Bilan* at the end writes a next-time note — what happened in this session, never a label — to paste into the next one.

## Provenance

Built from a real parent's request; everything identifying is left out. Illustrative example.

## A way, never the way

A football stadium is the way in here because this learner loves the game and running. The concepts — fractions as equal parts, comparing by common denominators, the whole found by inversion — are grounded in things the learner can see move in three dimensions. A different learner would reach fractions through something else entirely. Use what fits; leave the rest.
