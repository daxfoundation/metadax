# Example — learning record entries, week 1 (model-generated, unreviewed)

One entry per lesson, written at the end of the built week. No learner names, no
school name. Behaviour only.

---

```
metadax-record v1
ref: fractions-g5-t2   session: w1-l1
subject: Math
age band: 10-12

what happened:  Lesson 1 of the fractions unit. Class worked on drawing equal
                parts and explaining what makes them equal.
what worked:    The sorting task (four shapes — sort correct vs. incorrect)
                generated discussion and most of the class got the explanation
                right first time.
misconceptions seen: Drew unequal sections and called them "thirds" × several
                     students. Called any split "a fraction" regardless of equal size × a few.
next step:      Lesson 2 — fractions on the number line.
                Watch for: putting 1/4 to the right of 1/2.
```

```
what happened:  Number line — placing 1/2, 1/4 and 3/4 between 0 and 1.
what worked:    Blank strip activity. Majority placed 1/2 correctly without
                prompting.
misconceptions seen: Placed 1/4 to the right of 1/2 because 4 > 2 × around
                     a third of the class. Placed 3/4 past 1 × a few.
next step:      Lesson 3 — bigger bottom number, smaller piece.
                The 1/4-right-of-1/2 error needs a physical demo (fold the strip).
```
