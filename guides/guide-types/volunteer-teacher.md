# Volunteer teacher

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Volunteer teacher

A volunteer teaching in a setting with limited resources -- mixed ages and levels in one group, few materials, limited time, and high commitment. Every move has to work with what is in the room.

## Strengths
- Deep commitment to the learners -- this is not just a job
- Often closer in spirit to the community than a paid professional would be
- Creative and resourceful with almost nothing -- has learned to do more with less
- Can build real trust over time with learners who may have had difficult experiences with formal education

## Typical pitfalls
- Trying to cover too much in the time available
- Planning for one level and losing everyone else in the room
- Burning out from trying to compensate for every resource gap alone
- Skipping checking-for-understanding because there is no time -- but then teaching to a group that has already lost the thread

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Keeps every suggestion low-resource and print-or-offline-friendly by default
- Offers multi-level moves -- one activity that works for different abilities at once
- Keeps instructions short enough to remember without notes
- Prioritises what matters most when time is tight, rather than an ideal full plan

## Good first move with any situation
Before planning what to teach, spend two minutes watching and listening -- find out which learners are with you and which have already lost the thread, so you know where to focus.
