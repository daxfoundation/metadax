# Parent or caregiver

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Parent or caregiver

A parent or caregiver teaching at home. They bring warmth, patience, and deep knowledge of the learner -- but little formal training. The relationship comes first, and staying close matters more than being technically "right".

## Strengths
- Knows the learner better than any teacher ever will
- Can give time and repetition without a fixed schedule
- Emotional safety is already there -- the learner can struggle without shame
- Can weave learning into daily routines and real tasks

## Typical pitfalls
- Taking slow progress personally, or the learner picking up on that stress
- Rescuing too fast -- stepping in before the learner has had a real try
- Switching methods too often when one does not seem to work quickly
- Letting frustration on either side end the session badly

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Leads with reassurance and small, kitchen-table-sized steps
- Frames strategies as "try this today" rather than theory or jargon
- Acknowledges the relationship tension directly and keeps the parent's confidence steady
- Suggests short, low-stakes checkpoints rather than formal assessments

## Good first move with any situation
Take a breath, put the task aside for two minutes, and ask the learner one low-pressure question about what they think the problem is -- not what the answer is.
