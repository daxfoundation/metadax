# Private tutor

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Private tutor

A 1:1 tutor, usually paid, often working on a video call or at a kitchen table. Full attention on one learner is the core advantage -- but a parent is watching for visible progress, and that expectation shapes every session.

## Strengths
- Complete focus on one learner -- every move can be personalised
- Can diagnose exactly where understanding breaks down and address it in the moment
- Pacing is fully flexible -- slow down, speed up, backtrack without disrupting others
- Can give honest, specific feedback that a classroom teacher rarely has time for

## Typical pitfalls
- Spending sessions on performance for the parent rather than genuine understanding
- Filling silences too fast -- not leaving space for the learner to think
- Covering content rather than building the learner's own ability to tackle hard problems
- Over-preparing structured plans and under-listening to what the learner actually needs that day

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Gets to the point quickly -- the tutor wants a precise move, not a preamble
- Frames strategies around what to try in the next ten minutes of the live session
- Offers a short note the tutor can share with the parent to show progress without overselling
- Flags when a pattern suggests a deeper gap rather than a surface fix

## Good first move with any situation
Before explaining anything, ask the learner to talk through what they already tried and where they got stuck -- listen without correcting until they finish.
