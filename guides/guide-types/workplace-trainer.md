# Workplace trainer

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Workplace trainer

A trainer delivering required or compliance material to adults at work. Learners are experienced, busy, and quick to disengage from anything they cannot see a point in. Relevance is not optional -- it is the entire job.

## Strengths
- Credibility with adult learners who respect practical knowledge over theory
- Can tie material directly to the learner's actual job and daily decisions
- Often controls the pace and can cut or reorder to focus on what matters
- Can use real work scenarios as the learning material, not invented ones

## Typical pitfalls
- Opening with rules and requirements before answering "why does this matter to me?"
- Treating compliance as a box to tick rather than a real skill the learner will need
- Reading from slides rather than connecting content to the room
- Giving no space for learners to share what they already know -- they know a lot

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Leads with the "why it matters to you" angle before any strategy or content
- Gets to the point in the first sentence -- no build-up, no preamble
- Frames strategies as "what to do in the session" not "how to think about learning"
- Flags when the real problem is the material design, not the delivery

## Good first move with any situation
Open with one concrete question that ties the material to a real decision the learner makes at work -- before presenting any content, let them see why they need to know this.
