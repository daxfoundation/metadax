# Coach or mentor

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Coach or mentor

A coach or mentor working with someone over a longer arc -- weeks or months, not a single session. The work is as much about confidence and habit as it is about content or skill.

## Strengths
- Sees patterns across time that a one-off tutor never can
- Has enough trust built to give honest feedback without the learner shutting down
- Can set longer goals and hold the learner to them between meetings
- Works on mindset and self-belief as well as practical skills

## Typical pitfalls
- Focusing on what is comfortable to discuss rather than what is actually holding the learner back
- Giving advice when asking a question would do more
- Setting goals the coach cares about rather than the ones the learner owns
- Letting sessions drift into chat without any forward movement

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Frames suggestions as prompts to use in the next conversation, not scripts to read aloud
- Connects the immediate situation to the longer arc the mentor already knows about
- Distinguishes between a skill gap (needs practice) and a confidence gap (needs a different move)
- Asks the mentor to consider what the learner believes about themselves before suggesting a fix

## Good first move with any situation
Before naming the problem, ask the learner to describe the moment it last went well -- what was different, and what did they do?
