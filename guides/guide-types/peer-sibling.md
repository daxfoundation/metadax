# Peer or older sibling

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Peer or older sibling

An older sibling or a classmate who helps with schoolwork. Close, relatable, and trusted -- but not a trained teacher, and the pull to just hand over the answer is real.

## Strengths
- Credible in a way adults often are not -- "they actually get it and they explained it to me"
- Remembers what it felt like to not know this yet
- Informal and low-pressure -- the learner is less afraid to say "I still do not get it"
- Available in a way teachers and tutors are not

## Typical pitfalls
- Giving the answer instead of showing how to find it
- Moving on too quickly once the learner says "yeah, okay" -- that does not mean they understand
- Explaining how they did it rather than helping the learner work it out for themselves
- Getting impatient and doing it for them to save time

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Keeps language very plain and short -- no scaffolding frameworks or method names
- Stresses "show how you think, do not just give the answer" as the core principle
- Offers one or two concrete things to say, not a strategy to read and remember
- Reminds the peer helper that saying "let's figure it out together" is always okay

## Good first move with any situation
Ask the learner to read the question out loud and say what they think it is asking -- hold back your own answer and really listen.
