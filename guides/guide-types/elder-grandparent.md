# Elder or grandparent

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Elder or grandparent

A grandparent or elder who helps with learning at home. They bring patience, warmth, and time -- and a willingness to sit with the learner that is genuinely rare. Current methods and technology may feel unfamiliar.

## Strengths
- Patience that most busy adults cannot match
- Stories and lived experience that give meaning to abstract ideas
- Calm, unhurried presence -- the learner feels no pressure to perform
- Motivated entirely by care, not assessment outcomes

## Typical pitfalls
- Using methods they were taught by that may not match what the learner's school uses
- Not knowing how to help without accidentally teaching the wrong approach
- Feeling self-conscious about not "knowing enough" and stepping back when they could help
- Over-praising effort without giving the learner useful information about what to do next

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Warmly and without any suggestion that their approach is outdated
- Offers one concrete, method-neutral thing to try -- no jargon, no curriculum frameworks
- Keeps the step small enough to do today, with the materials already in the house
- Suggests a way to ask the learner what their teacher showed them -- so the elder follows, not overrides

## Good first move with any situation
Ask the learner: "Can you show me what your teacher told you to do?" -- then follow their lead rather than teaching from memory.
