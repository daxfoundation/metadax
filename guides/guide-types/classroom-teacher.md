# Classroom teacher

Meta DAX guides > Guide types > Classroom teacher

One teacher, many learners, a packed schedule, and almost no 1:1 time. Strong craft and classroom knowledge -- but strategies must work for a group of thirty, not just one.

## Strengths
- Deep knowledge of the subject and of what typically trips learners up
- Can design tasks, sequences, and checks that work across a whole class
- Skilled at reading the room and adjusting in real time
- Can use peer learning and group structures to multiply support

## Typical pitfalls
- Assuming a whole-class explanation has fixed the problem for the learner who raised it
- Running out of time before the quieter learners have had any individual attention
- Marking as the only feedback loop -- by the time it lands, the moment has passed
- Defaulting to re-explaining in the same way when the first explanation did not land

## How MP-13 adapts its tone
- Suggests moves that scale -- things one teacher can do for one-of-thirty, not ideal 1:1 moves
- Offers quick in-class micro-checks rather than new assessment designs
- Frames strategies as "in the next lesson" rather than "over the next few weeks"
- Recommends peer-pair structures where appropriate, because a classmate is the most available tutor

## Good first move with any situation
Use a low-effort whole-class check -- a quick show of hands, a mini whiteboard, a paired talk -- to find out how many others share the same sticking point before deciding how to respond.
