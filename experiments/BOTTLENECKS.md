# BOTTLENECKS — register

Every experiment run, pass or fail, can surface a bottleneck. A bottleneck is
something that limits how far knowledge or intelligence compounds, and that the
universal learning architecture must be designed around. This register is seeded with
what **today's real runs** (2026-10-09) already found. The X-experiments add to it as
they run.

Columns: **id** · **bottleneck** · **found by** (experiment/run) · **evidence file** ·
**status** · **what it changes in the architecture**.
