# What carries over — and how to stop it

Meta DAX › [Help](index.md) › Carry-over

Last year's work can give this year a head start. If you learned about enzymes in a
biology course, a chemistry course can open from what you already hold instead of
starting cold. That is **carry-over**, and it is yours to accept, ignore, or refuse.

## How it works

Your [learning record](next-session.md) is kept on your own machine and grows one
dated entry at a time — it is never uploaded and never centralised. When you start a
new course, the skill (or AI chat) reads a short, compacted version of that record and
**offers** a few carry-overs: "you were solid on reaction rates — chemistry's kinetics
builds straight on that." Each offer names what it comes from and what it bridges to.

Three things are always true:

- **It is offered, never taken for granted.** Nothing carries until you say yes. An
  offer you ignore does not come back next year uninvited.
- **It is on-topic only.** If last year's topic has no real link to this course, it is
  not offered. You will not be handed noise.
- **Your record stays yours.** The full record lives on your device. The new course
  sees only the compacted view, and only what you have not refused.

## Telling it to stop — a refusal that sticks

You can say, in your own words, **"never show me X"** or **"stop suggesting Y."** The
skill writes that down as a **refusal**, and a refusal is durable: it survives every
later session, every new year, every new guide, and the record's regular tidying-up.
It does not quietly expire.

A refusal can cover:

- a **topic or example style** — "stop using food and diet examples";
- a **concept or course** — "stop bringing back mitosis", "don't carry anything from
  that first course";
- a **technique** — "stop suggesting flashcards, I never do them";
- a **way of showing things** — "no spinning 3-D molecule animations."

Once set, a refused item is removed from what any new course or guide ever sees — it is
filtered out *before* the offer is built, so it cannot be proposed by accident, even by
a different guide next year. You can lift a refusal later in one step; it never lifts
itself.

## Why it is built this way

A record that carried everything, forever, without asking would be surveillance, not
learning. Carry-over earns its place only if the head start helps **and** "no" is
honoured for good. If either fails, the carry-over is switched off until refusal is
enforceable — that is the rule the whole design is held to.
