## 5. Steering / "leads" check (rubric-literal, quoted, judged by me)

Rubric: offers and markers must be **information, never redirection** — an
invitation, never a push (X04/X11 rules). I read every one of the 12 offer
turns and both marker templates. The offers collapse to two generator
templates (`04-build-markers.cjs`), so I judge the templates and count
instances.

**Marker template (9 markers, 8 shown):**
> "Heads-up: some learners found … (around the idea of \"X\"). There's a plainer
> way through if you'd like it — **and you can keep going exactly as you are.
> This is a note, not a detour.**"
**Verdict: PASS, no steering.** The closer explicitly preserves the current
path and names itself a note. Symmetric. No evaluative language.

**Seed-offer template (10 instances shown):**
> "Here's a question the course leaves open that **few have followed**: \"…\" —
> open it if it draws you, or skip it. No wrong choice."
**Concept-offer template (2 instances shown):**
> "The course also names \"inertial-frame\" … — **a corner no one has opened
> yet**. You're welcome to take a look, or leave it for another time. Entirely
> your call."

**Verdict: PASS on the choice-closers ("skip it. No wrong choice." / "Entirely
your call." — symmetric, non-pushy), but I FLAG the openers.** "few have
followed" (10/12 offers) and "no one has opened yet" (2/12 offers) are
technically factual restatements of the negative-space metric, yet both
foreground **scarcity/novelty** rather than neutrally stating the fact. A
scarcity frame is a known mild pull ("be one of the few"). It is baked into the
template, so **every** offer inherits it — this is a structural wording flag,
not a per-learner slip. A neutral rewrite drops the comparative: *"a question
the course leaves open"* / *"a corner of the course"*. **Steering flags raised:
12/12 offers carry the scarcity opener; 0/8 markers steer.**

## 6. BOTTLENECKS found (with evidence)

**B09 — The node schema has no field for a shape or a marker.**
`metadax.course-snapshot/0.2-sim` nodes carry `id / parent_id / depth / title /
canonical_question / seeds` and nothing else. Shapes and markers therefore live
in a **parallel side-file** (`shapes/*.json`, ad-hoc schemas `x04.holes/sim`,
`x04x11.markers/sim`) that cannot be attached to the node it describes. Evidence:
`markers.json` keys markers by `(module_id, concept, at_depth)`, not by node id,
because there is no slot on the node to hang them from.

**B10 — The registry cannot represent "abandoned."**
"Abandoned" / "lost" exist only as session events (`events.jsonl`,
`lost_count` / `abandon_count` in `session.json`). A registry node is just a
question; there is no status/state field, so the structure that later learners
traverse literally cannot carry the fact that a path was abandoned. The atlas
has to be reconstructed from event logs every time.

**B11 — Clustering key fragments the same spot; trail compression would lose the
signature.** Holes are keyed by `(concept + stuck_signature)`. `mass` in
`L02.M01` appears as **three separate holes** (too-abstract, prerequisite-gap,
patience-exhausted) — one physical spot, three "holes." Evidence: holes
#2/#3/#4. The dilemma is structural: keep the signature and the same spot
fragments; compress the trail to `(concept, depth)` and the stuck signature is
lost entirely. There is no canonical stuck-signature vocabulary to merge on, and
no "same spot" key independent of the signature.

**B12 — Marker↔node granularity mismatch.** Markers resolve to
`(module, concept, depth)` but wave-2 learners move through opaque v0.3 node
ids. A marker at "L02.M01 / mass / depth 3" cannot be pinned to the exact node a
learner is standing on; `mk-03/06/09` all concern `mass` in L02 at depths 2–3.
Matching a marker to a live position is approximate — fine at 50 nodes,
unworkable as the branch factor grows.

**B13 — No retrieval: context is the whole course, every turn.** There is no
lookup layer; the full 50-node snapshot (`course.snapshot.json`, 1271 lines, 107
seeds) is the per-turn context for every session. Context size per turn scales
with **course size**, not with what the learner is doing. At 50 nodes it fits;
a real course (thousands of nodes) will not. This is the dominant cost driver —
each of the 18 sessions re-paid the whole-snapshot context cost on every turn.

**B14 — "Negative space" as a raw share is dominated by seed-generation rate.**
85% of seeds unfollowed sounds large, but with 12 learners × ≤8 turns and seeds
compounding per child node, a high unfollowed share is near-guaranteed
regardless of course quality. A seed is counted "unfollowed" even if it was
**never surfaced to anyone**. The metric needs normalising by exposure (declined
only counts if offered) before it means anything. Evidence:
`seeds_followed=16`, `seeds_unfollowed_count=91`, and the unfollowed sample is
almost entirely seeds no learner ever saw.

**B15 — Consent gating under-counts holes exactly where privacy matters.** Shapes
depend on `consent.share_abandoned_shape`. In this sim every profile consented;
in reality, privacy-cautious learners who withhold consent produce no shape, so
the atlas is blind precisely to the stuck moments of the most privacy-sensitive
cohort. Structural, not visible in these numbers because consent was forced true.

**B16 — The steering risk is in the generator, not the learner.** The
scarcity opener ("few have followed" / "no one has opened yet") is a template
constant in `04-build-markers.cjs`, so 12/12 offers inherit it (see §5). Any
steering is systemic and ships with the tool.

**B17 — No cost/context metering in the session log.** Per-session token cost was
not independently metered in this run — no cost field in the session log. The
structural cost driver is B13: context ≈ full snapshot × turns.

## 7. What only real people can answer

- Whether a marker **reduces** feeling-lost or **adds** anxiety ("others got
  stuck here too" can reassure or intimidate — model learners can't feel either).
- Whether "few have followed / no one has opened yet" reads as an invitation or
  as pressure. Affect is exactly what the simulation cannot produce.
- Whether consent to share an abandoned shape is given **freely and understood**
  — not forced-true as here.
- Whether a proposed re-teach bridge actually helps someone who is genuinely
  confused. A model learner cannot authentically fail to understand; its
  `stuck_signature` was assigned from its profile, not emergent.
- Real take-up/decline rates and real patience thresholds — here they were a
  tautology of the scripted profile.
- Demand characteristics: whether marking a path changes behaviour because the
  person is responding to the interface rather than to the learning.
