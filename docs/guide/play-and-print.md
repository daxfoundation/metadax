# Open and print a MetaDAX package

MetaDAX › [Help](index.md) › Open & print

Once you have a package — the `metadax-package` JSON from the skill or an AI chat
— the player opens it. One shared player renders every package. Everything runs in
your browser.

## Open it in the player

1. Go to the player: [daxfoundation.org/metadax/play/](https://daxfoundation.org/metadax/play/).
2. Paste your package JSON into "Open your package" and press **Play**.
3. You get two tabs: **For you** (the guide) and **For the learner** (your child's
   side, with the game). Read your page first.

The player says it plainly: *"Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere."* It works
offline once the page has loaded — you can save the player page and drop the JSON
into it for a fully offline file.

## Print the learner page and the guide key

Press **Print this**. The printed version is paper-first and in black and white,
and it prints two things in order:

- **The learner page** — the part your child works from. The games become
  questions with a box to tick and a line to write on. No answers are shown here.
- **The guide key** — on its own page at the end. It carries the answer to each
  question and one line of why. As it says: *"Keep this page; the learner page
  above has none of it."*

So you can hand your child their pages and keep the guide key yourself. Every sheet
carries the same two lines at the foot: *behaviour never diagnoses*, and *a way,
never the way*.

## The "say it, then find it" item

Some lessons — early reading especially — include a spoken-sound item. **You say
the prompt aloud; your child taps or circles the one they hear.** The page itself
speaks nothing. On screen the answer is marked as a preview for you; in print the
answer stays off the learner page and sits only in the guide key.

## Companion notes (for the guide only)

A package can come with optional **companion notes** — a small sidecar file. If you
have one, open "Companion notes" in the player and paste it in. Only its
guide-facing notes are shown, and only on your "For you" tab — never on your
child's side, and never in print. If the notes don't match the expected shape, the
player simply plays the package without them.
