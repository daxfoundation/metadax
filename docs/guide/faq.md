# Meta DAX for parents — questions and answers

Meta DAX › [Help](index.md) › Questions

Plain answers to the things parents ask first.

### Is it free?

Yes, and it stays free. If you build the lesson yourself, it runs on an AI
subscription you already pay for; the project is not charging you. If you use the
web questionnaire instead, that costs nothing at all.

### Does it save anything about us?

The player keeps nothing and sends nothing. In its own words: "Nothing here is
saved or sent anywhere," and "Everything runs in your browser." The lesson file
stays on your device. If you save a session to a learning record, that file
downloads to your device too — nothing is uploaded. The one thing that ever leaves
is a generic sample, and only if you choose to share one.

### What ages is it for?

Roughly ages 4 to 18. A child uses their side with you beside them — it is built
for an adult to run, not for a child on their own. There is no AI on the child's
side and it names no tool.

### Does it replace our curriculum?

No. It is one way in for one child on one stuck spot — a way, never the way. Use it
alongside whatever you already do, for the parts where something keeps going wrong.

### What if my child is really struggling?

Describe what your child does — "covers the page after two tries," "says the sounds
but they stay apart" — and the lesson is built around that behaviour. Meta DAX
never labels a child and never names a condition, anywhere. If you are worried, the
right next step is to talk to a professional who can do an evaluation; the lesson
will say so plainly when the signs warrant it, and it names nothing.

### Do I need to be good at the subject myself?

No. Your page is written for where you are — it tells you what to say, what to skip,
which slips are normal, and one line of why under each step. The part only you can
do is notice your child and decide when to push on or stop.

### Which AI do I need?

Any capable assistant you already pay for. There is a ready-made skill for
assistants that read a SKILL.md, and a plain prompt for everything else. The task
uses nothing specific to one product, and no AI vendor is named on your child's
side.

### Is there an app to install?

No. The player is a web page. You can open it online, or save the page and use it
offline. There is no account and no sign-up.

### Can I print it instead of using a screen?

Yes. The player's "Print this" gives you the learner page to work from and, on its
own page at the end, a guide key with the answers and one line of why. Hand your
child their pages; keep the key.

### Can I teach more than one child from it?

Yes. Answer the questions once for the group, giving each child a nickname and a
rough age, and the package carries each of them — one reads a part, the little ones
do the hands-on part, the oldest does the explaining part.

### What happens if I share a sample?

Only a generic version goes out — made-up names, nothing identifying — and only
when you choose. You post it through a form, and a person reviews it before it ever
appears in the public library.

### Who makes it?

The DAX Foundation, in the open. Meta DAX is a research project, built in public on
GitHub; what runs and what has not is all published there.
