# The generic sample format

A sample is a short markdown file another guide can read, with **made-up** guide
and learner names and **nothing** identifying. It is built from a real family's
package only with that family's agreement, and it keeps the teaching while
dropping every private detail.

## Frontmatter — the nine index fields

```yaml
---
id: subject-topic-hook        # kebab-case, unique; e.g. reading-blending-dinosaurs
subject: Reading              # Math | Reading | Writing | Spelling | Science | French | Language | History | Other
age_band: "7-9"               # one of: "4-6" "4-9" "7-9" "10-12" "13-15" "16-18"
stuck: "says each sound but can't push them together into a word"   # behaviour, short, no diagnosis
loves: "dinosaurs"
approach: "blends sounds by stomping out dinosaur names, one sound per step"  # one line
date: "2026-10-09"
licence: "CC BY 4.0"
path: "samples/reading-blending-dinosaurs.md"
---
```

Keep `stuck` behavioural and never a diagnosis. Keep every field one short line.

## Body — these headings, in order

```
# <Title> — a Meta DAX homeschool sample

*A made-up example built from a parent's description. A way, never the way.*

**Guide:** <fictional name> · **Learner:** <fictional name> · **Age band:** … · **Subject:** …

## Where the learner was stuck
## The approach (built through what they love)
## For the guide: the shape of a session
## What only a person can do
## Where the learner leads
## The game on the learner's side
## When to seek an evaluation
## A way, never the way
```

Fill each section with 2–6 sentences or a short list, drawn from the package.
"When to seek an evaluation" names behaviours a guide might watch for and says to
consider asking a professional for an evaluation — it **names no diagnosis and no
condition**. No AI or vendor names, no links, no emoji, no real names, no dates in
the prose (the frontmatter date is fine).
